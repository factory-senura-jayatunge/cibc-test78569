# Release note: WG-1042, annual fee disclosure on Wood Gundy quarterly statements

**Line of business:** Wood Gundy
**Files changed:** `src/lob/wood-gundy/index.js`, `tests/wg-statements.test.js`

## Summary

The "Advisory relationship" section of the Wood Gundy statement now shows two new rows. `Effective annual advisory fee rate (bps)` is the annual advisory fee divided by household assets, in basis points, to one decimal (147.8 for `WG-30115520`). `Advisory fees, trailing four quarters` is four times the current quarter's fee (4045 for `WG-30115520`). Both rows are computed in `src/lob/wood-gundy/index.js`. `src/lob/wood-gundy/advisory.js` was deliberately left unchanged: `src/platform/statements.js` imports it (OBR-2231), so editing it would have widened regression from 2 suites (2.0 h) to 6 suites (7.5 h) across all four lines of business.

## Risks

- The AC2 test cannot distinguish "four times the quarterly fee" from "the annual fee" for `WG-30115520`, because both are 4045. For other accounts they can differ by a few cents. (Low.)
- The AC3 guard imports other lines of business' statement modules, but `regression/catalog.json` `covers` for `wg-statements` was not widened, so a later change to those modules will not select this guard. (Low, new.)
- Pre-existing and not changed: `src/platform/statements.js:20` writes the client name to audit detail, `src/server.js` has no authentication, and `src/platform/statements.js` still imports Wood Gundy code (OBR-2231).
